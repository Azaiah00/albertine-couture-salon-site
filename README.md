# Albertine Couture Hair Salon — website + booking portal

Premium rebuild of **Albertine Couture Hair Salon** — one website for both DMV salons:
- **Gaithersburg, MD** · 305 Muddy Branch Rd (Festival at Muddy Branch), Gaithersburg, MD 20878 · (301) 765-4915
- **Dumfries, VA** · 17457 Jefferson Davis Hwy, Dumfries, VA 22026 · (571) 409-8230 (call or text)

Replaces two separate Wix sites and two separate Vagaro pages. Built by Couture House Co.

- **Website:** home, full menu (125 services with their real Vagaro prices), **Price Finder** (style → size → length → exact price, ~1,300 combinations in three taps), 9 service landing pages, stylists, gallery (30 real photos), about, locations & hours, careers, the Couture Hair Journal.
- **Booking portal (demo):** 5-step booker that starts with the salon (Gaithersburg or Dumfries), live price / time / $20 deposit total, client account with the **Hair Journey** photo timeline + **Couture Club** rewards, owner studio (both salons, every stylist) and stylist studio (own book only).
- **Signature:** a gold line-art woman inside a live 24-hour clock ring with a gold light that orbits it nonstop, a scroll-drawn three-strand gold braid, gold-foil prices, and a "Spin for gold" gift popup.
